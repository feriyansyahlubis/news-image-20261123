A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of **Monday, 23 November 2026** in one image: `entry.png` (1024×1024, rendered from `news.html`).

## The six stories

1. **Russian warship sinks off Libya** — the corvette Merkury capsized at Benghazi naval base after a suspected Ukrainian sea-drone strike; ten hurt, a second ship hit hours later.
2. **Philippines counts 8,320 drug-war dead** — the independent Truth Commission presents its first report in Manila: 8,320 killings documented from 2016 to 2022.
3. **Trump: strikes on Iran to resume after New Year if no deal** — the US president tells Fox News he is "inclined" to resume strikes and warns blockade-breakers off.
4. **Nepal's PM holds talks with Modi in New Delhi** — Balendra Shah's first bilateral trip abroad since March, with a joint vision document on the table.
5. **Seoul minister quits over AI exam-cheating chaos** — education minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns after last-minute anti-cheating rules for the CSAT sowed confusion; 111 suspected cases caught.
6. **Liverpool 4–0 Man Utd: Pochettino's debut disaster** — Yoro sees red after eight minutes; the joint-heaviest defeat for a United boss in his first game since 1926.

Every fact was verified against live reporting (AP, Reuters, Al Jazeera, The Times, IANS) on 23 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) checks that `entry.png` is a real PNG by its magic bytes, then copies it to `/output/entry.png`. Two runs write identical bytes.
