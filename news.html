Libya · The Mediterranean

Russian warship sinks off Libya after suspected Ukrainian drone strike

The corvette Merkury capsized and sank at Benghazi naval base after two explosive sea drones struck her at dawn; ten people were hurt and a second, Maltese-flagged ship was hit hours later. Satellite images showed most of the warship under water, only her bow above the surface, and Libyan forces said they intercepted more than 20 drone boats off Benghazi on Sunday evening. Libya’s eastern government has asked the United Nations to investigate the attack, which carries the Ukraine war more than 1,000 miles from the Black Sea.

Philippines · Justice

Manila counts 8,320 drug-war dead

The independent Truth Commission presents its first report today: 8,320 killings documented from 2016 to 2022, 499 of them still unnamed, against the police count of 6,252. The dead were overwhelmingly poor urban men — 96.6 percent of the victims — and the commission proposes 27 reforms, a week before Duterte’s trial opens at the ICC.

United States · Iran

Trump: strikes on Iran to resume after New Year if no deal

The US president tells Fox News he is “inclined” to resume strikes and warns ships testing the naval blockade will end up “at the bottom of the sea” — while seeking a “permanent” deal to stop Iran getting a nuclear weapon. The blockade of Iranian ports, reimposed in July, remains in force.

India · Nepal

Nepal’s PM holds talks with Modi in New Delhi

Balendra Shah, on his first bilateral trip abroad since taking office in March, meets Narendra Modi to review trade, energy and connectivity and unveil a joint vision document, with new agreements on health, education and agriculture. He arrived with a 35-member government delegation and a 52-member business delegation.

South Korea · Education

Seoul minister quits over AI exam-cheating chaos

Education minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns after last-minute anti-cheating rules for the CSAT college entrance exam sowed confusion across the country. The president accepted it within the hour; officials caught 111 suspected cases of cheating with electronic devices. The trigger was the top scorer in a tax-accountant exam admitting he cheated with a miniature camera, a smartphone and AI.

England · Football

Liverpool 4–0 Man Utd: Pochettino’s debut disaster

Leny Yoro sees red after eight minutes and Liverpool cruise home at Anfield — Szoboszlai, Wirtz, Ngumoha and Muñoz score in a 4–0 rout, the joint-heaviest defeat for a United boss in his first game since 1926. United sit 19th with one win from 11 games; Pochettino arrived only on Wednesday after coaching the United States national team.
